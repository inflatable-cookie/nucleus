A run-bound build output disclosure now names what its cleanup record observed
from that record's own typed field, so a reworded detail sentence can no longer
change what Nucleus says a cleanup kept or removed. A record written before the
field existed keeps the labels its sentence already gave a continuation.

A board card's waiting prerequisite is decoded by the reason's own owner, and a
blocker escalation names the planner or owner it waits with from a typed field on
the stop record. A stop recorded before the field existed keeps its named planner
or task owner. The stop-reason families the task quantum classifies from an
agent's own report are one declared precedence table with a named attention tail,
and the card asks the stop owner whether a recorded stop is a wait or a hold
instead of classifying its detail itself.

The `shared/question-caller.mjs` compatibility re-export is deleted; its two
tests import `callerSource` from `shared/caller.mjs` directly.
