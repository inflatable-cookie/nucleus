A planner's own hold now parks the task in T3 instead of leaving its row visible.
An effective hold whose holder is the task's planner snoozes the task row whether or
not it names a release condition, while a running role, an independent call for a
person, a fault or a pending question or permission keeps the row visible; a hold
from the operator's board or from another participant keeps its existing attention. The three-hour hold nudge now opens a thirty-minute
answer window: releasing the hold or keeping it into a fresh generation answers it,
and a window that closes with the hold still in place shows the task row again and
records that the nudge went unanswered — or, when delivery was never confirmed, that
it never reached the planner and why. A timer never releases, lifts or changes a
hold, and a self-lifting hold is never measured this way.
