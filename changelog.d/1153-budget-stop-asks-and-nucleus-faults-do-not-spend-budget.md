A budget stop is never silent: it raises its question in every phase and names
`extend_budget` among the owner verbs that answer it, whatever the task's phase or
host state (a host that is offline records the question on the board instead of
dropping it). Wall time now charges only the task's own work: time the task spends
held on a Nucleus-side cause — a host-adapter refusal or missing workspace, no
runnable profile, a provider outage, a dispatch hold, or a wedged sweep on its lane
— is paused in a durable interval, subtracted from the budget and named on the
budget's time bar.
