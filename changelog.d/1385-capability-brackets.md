Profile capability is now four brackets: Mechanical, Worker, Heavyweight and
Frontier. Medium and high profiles form one Worker pool; a medium task stays on
Worker, while a high task starts there and may step up once to Heavyweight after
review changes. The reviewer runs one bracket below the worker at Low and Normal
and on the worker's bracket above that, and the coordinator runs on Mechanical
when one is available. Settings → Role demand is gone. Stored ratings move to
the brackets once on open, and stored role offsets are dropped and noted.
