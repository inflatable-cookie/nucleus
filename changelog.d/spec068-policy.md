Budgets, caps and the third review round follow the approved self-healing policy (Spec 068 Decisions 1 and 2). Time while the Nucleus service is down, rolling or asleep with the machine no longer spends a task's budget, alongside host outages. A task's first spent budget is extended once automatically, in a phase whose next step is a run, and recorded as an automatic `budget_extended`; only a later spend stops the task and asks, and only the planner or the operator board may start a run past it. A revision launch past the cap now holds however the task reached revision, until the planner or the operator accepts another round. A spent budget or exhausted revision cap now shows as a typed stop with its explicit command instead of "progressing", and the `run.start` budget warning uses the scheduler's own cap condition. The third non-converging review round continues automatically and tells the task's planner the reviewer's findings instead of asking; a tell that sent nothing (a service stop, or no planner or host yet) is retried on a later pass. The coordinator loop bound now charges only coordinator runs that changed nothing, with a ceiling of six launches per head. Explicit commands print their outcome first, name the warnings only a planner or the operator board may accept, and exit non-zero unless they applied; `next` lists each command's expected warnings.
