- A recovery coordinator's report that names no owner verb — "live, no action",
  or a `cannot` with a reason — settles its recovery episode as an observation
  instead of being refused as a retired narrow action. The episode resolves with
  the report's own words, nothing is refused, no needs-attention notice is raised,
  and the unchanged stop and work identity keep a later pass from opening another
  coordinator. A `cannot` also opens the planner-ladder question with its reason,
  because someone else must act; a live no-action report asks nobody. A
  coordinator can report the outcome explicitly as `{verb: "none", reason: "no
  action: <who> is live and progressing"}`, and the recovery prompt and rules name
  both outcomes. Only a stored retired narrow action is still refused as retired,
  and its refusal names the owner verb that replaced it.
