- A recovery coordinator's report that names no owner verb — "live, no action",
  or a blocked/error report saying it cannot act — settles its recovery episode
  as an observation instead of being refused as a retired narrow action. The
  episode resolves with the report's own words, nothing is refused or escalated,
  no needs-attention notice is raised, and the unchanged stop and work identity
  keep a later pass from opening another coordinator. A coordinator can report the
  outcome explicitly as `{verb: "none", reason: "no action: <who> is live and
  progressing"}`, and the recovery prompt and rules name it. Only a stored retired
  narrow action is still refused as retired, and its refusal now names the owner
  verb that replaced it.
