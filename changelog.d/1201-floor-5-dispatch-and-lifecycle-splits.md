Dispatch, thread choice, required-check waits and the terminal workspace archive are
split along their seams, and each resulting module is the single owner of its
mechanism. The required-check wait is now identified by its durable poll record
instead of the wording of the task's reason, its escalation is matched by a
recorded refusal class instead of the revision instruction text, and
unrevisioned gate evidence is current only while the repository still has no
settings row. A workspace archive's teardown/retirement failure classification
has one owner. No product behaviour, contract, CLI or wire shape changes.
