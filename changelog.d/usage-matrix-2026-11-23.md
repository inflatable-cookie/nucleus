Usage levels follow the 2026-11-23 matrix. Maximum no longer puts every worker
and reviewer on Heavyweight or Frontier: it starts very-low and low work on
Worker, medium and high on Heavyweight and very high on Frontier, and a high
task may climb once to Frontier after review changes. At High a Worker-tier
worker now gets a Worker reviewer; the one-bracket step down applies only to
Heavyweight and Frontier workers. No reviewer runs above its worker, and a
Mechanical worker gets a Mechanical reviewer at every level.
