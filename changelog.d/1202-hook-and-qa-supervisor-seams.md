Repository hook execution and milestone QA supervision are now split along their seams, with no behaviour change. The hook runner keeps its public front in `core/hooks/hooks.ts`, and one owner now holds each mechanism behind it: occurrence records, read-only execution, integration-write publication, restart reconciliation, retry and disposal proofs, the retention gate, and the Queue-owned checkout and integration base. Milestone QA's detached supervisor became the pipeline, the teardown retry, the detached executors and the run's worktree; its escapee reaping moved beside the other escapee custody, and the manifest worktree block is parsed once for both its readers. A hook restart now records an unreadable control manifest as its own explicit uncertainty instead of reporting a stale pin.
