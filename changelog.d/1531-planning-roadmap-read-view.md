- Planning reads as a roadmap. Projects are listed as Active, Idle on purpose or Dormant, each with one status line in words. A project's plan shows its focus (the first Now lane's aim), then Now, Next and Later as columns of lane cards with their aim, live work by phase, attention count and finish line. A lane opens as its own page with a breadcrumb back: the finish line and why it is still open, the latest status, what is in flight, what is waiting to start, what finished recently, and the document and plans it belongs to. Plan version numbers, step counters and empty tabs leave the main view: History pages back through every saved plan version, See all pages through a lane's whole finished history, and Details and Edit plan keep the rest.
