The board project selector and the Planning tab now share one activity rule for the Dormant group. A project is active when at least two distinct work items have had activity in the last three days, counting task submissions, progress and completions and planning records, each once however many edits or linked records it has. A single recent sweep task, a quiet project, and a project with only background activity stay Dormant. Parked projects keep their own group, and a registered planner does not decide the group.
