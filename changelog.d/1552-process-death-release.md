A stalled participant whose own recorded process is provably gone is released and
relaunched without waiting for an owner verb. After a reboot or a reused PID,
Nucleus records the proof, releases that run through the ordinary `run.release`
plan and lets ordinary admission continue: a reviewer gets a fresh thread, a
revision continues on the retained worker thread, and the initial worker returns
to ordinary dispatch on its retained worktree. An unreadable process table or a
live process proves nothing, so the run keeps its lease. A `run_stalled` stop also
admits the task's coordinator without waiting for its own subject to look idle.
