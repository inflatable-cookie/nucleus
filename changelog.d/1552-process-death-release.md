A stalled participant whose own recorded process is provably gone is released and
relaunched without waiting for an owner verb. After a reboot or a reused PID,
Nucleus records the proof, releases that run through the ordinary `run.release`
plan and lets ordinary admission relaunch the role. An unreadable process table
or a live process proves nothing, so the run keeps its lease. A `run_stalled`
stop also admits the task's coordinator without waiting for its own subject to
look idle.
