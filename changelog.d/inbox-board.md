### Changed

- The board is now an inbox: a menu (Needs you, Planning, Leads, Queue and Active with Working, In review and Closing), the open view's list, and the open record in a detail pane. Medium-width panels open records in a modal, and narrow ones fold the menu into a drawer. Planning shows its plan in the list pane and the lane in the detail pane (or a modal).
- The header is one row: the project selector sits beside Settings, Refresh and the Pause split button, and the status line is gone. Run state shows as a pill only when something needs noticing, and slot usage moved into the queue menu.
- Leads and papercuts are two tabs in the Leads view; the tag filter narrows the list and its tab counts, while the menu keeps the inbox total.
- The QA runs tab is no longer on the board.
