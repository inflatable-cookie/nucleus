Leads and papercuts can be parked: taken off the to-do list with a reason and an optional day to check again. A parked lead stays open and sits under a collapsed "Parked (N)" row at the end of its list, out of the counts. When its day comes, it returns to the list as "Due for a recheck" and the project planner hears about it once. Park and Unpark are in the lead and papercut detail. Triage's defer now parks the record with its revisit time, and each lead tagged `hold` becomes a parked lead with no date.
