Inbox lists read like a sidebar: rows have no divider lines, hover and keyboard
focus dim the row, and the open record keeps the selected surface. The menu
entries hover the same way. Only the title stays bold — the line 1 label reads
muted at normal weight and the status word reads in its tone. The single-kind
Leads and Papercuts views drop the kind word from line 1, and their pages grow
to twenty-five cards with a fill that survives view switches.
